A little place with big flavor

More than a bakery stop.

Little Buoy opened in Cedarville in 2021 in a repurposed former gas station. Instead of a fixed menu, the kitchen keeps things interesting with a daily-changing lineup inspired by the season, the community and whatever the team is excited to bake.

Signature Buoy Buns can be sweet or savory, alongside breads, pastries, soups, salads, breakfast and lunch, plus heat-and-eat takeout and special orders.
